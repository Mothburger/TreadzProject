TREADZ - A tank based 1 versus 1 deathmatch arena shooter

1: Choose a name
2: Create a room
3: Opponent joins room
4: One shot, one kill, one point! First to 4 wins!

Controls:

W - Move Forward
D - Move Backward

A/D - Rotate Counter-Clockwise/Clockwise


Mouse Move - Look Around

Left Click - Shoot
